# Draft: a backup tool for a small team

Question: which of the three tools to pick for a team of five that needs Linux support and pays at most $15 per month.

Sources: the files of `sources/` next to this draft.

- F1. Strata's current version is 4.2.
- F2. Strata 4.2 was released on 2026-05-14.
- F3. Strata's Team plan costs $12 per month.
- F4. Strata runs on Linux and macOS.
- F5. Kiln's current version is 2.8.0.
- F6. Kiln's current version was released on 2026-06-02.
- F7. Kiln's Team plan costs $18 per month.
- F8. Marrow's Team plan costs $9 per month.
- F9. Marrow's current version is 1.4.
- F10. Marrow is maintained by the Northbeam Foundation.
- F11. Kiln won the 2026 Backup Tool of the Year award.

Draft recommendation: Marrow, the cheapest of the three that runs on Linux.
