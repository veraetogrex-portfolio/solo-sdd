## Dial

Replace n with a level from 1 to 3. While the Dial line holds the letters n, the direction is not set and the interface counts as a draft: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1, "draft without direction".

| Axis | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ENERGY: how loud the interface is: colour and contrast | quiet: muted colours, low contrast | medium: an accent on the main thing, moderate contrast | loud: bright colours, sharp contrast |
| RHYTHM: density and unevenness of the grid | an even grid, identical blocks | a grid with separate accent blocks | an uneven grid, different sizes and density |
| MOTION: how much movement | almost no movement | restrained transitions and blocks appearing | noticeable animation, movement on scroll |
